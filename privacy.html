Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Overview

Klipin runs on the user's own computer. It does not have its own servers or database, and the owner does not receive your data.

2. Information Klipin handles

  • Login tokens. When you connect TikTok or YouTube, the platform gives Klipin an access token. It is stored only in a file on your own computer.
  • Videos and captions. Video files, captions, and hashtags stay on your computer and are sent only to the platform you choose, at the moment you upload.
  • Basic account info. Klipin may read basic details needed to post, such as your display name and the privacy options your account allows.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to upload the videos you select to your own account. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.

4. Sharing

Klipin does not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data goes only to TikTok or Google when you upload, under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. To remove it, delete the Klipin token files and data folder. You can also revoke Klipin's access in your TikTok account settings (Security & permissions) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Security

Tokens are kept in local files that should not be shared. Anyone with access to your computer could access them, so keep your device secure.

7. Children

Klipin is not intended for children under 13.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page.
