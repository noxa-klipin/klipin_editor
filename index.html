Klipin

A personal video publishing tool.

Klipin is a small tool that runs on the owner's own computer. It helps a creator prepare short-form videos they have made themselves and upload them to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts, with a caption and hashtags written by the creator.

What it does

The creator logs in to their own account through the official TikTok or Google login screen, picks a video from a folder on their computer, writes the caption, and sends it to their account. Nothing is posted without the creator choosing the video and confirming the upload.

Who it is for

Klipin is built for personal use by its owner. It is not a public service and does not collect accounts or content from other people.
